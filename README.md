The Herbalist's Adventure

CSC 231 Project 2: Platformer MVP (Level 1), by Elena Harik


Character

Name: Elizabeth Breyer

Backstory: Elizabeth Breyer has always been better with herbs than with blades. Her little shop sells potions, remedies and dried herbs to the whole village, and every ingredient comes from the forest nearby. But lately the forest has changed. Angry mushrooms walk the paths and strange flying creatures guard the trees. So Elizabeth taught herself to use a sword. Now she ventures into the forest to gather the herbs she needs, fights off anything that gets in her way, and returns home through the old portal before nightfall.

Goal: Collect all 6 herbs and reach the portal. You have 3 lives, and the campfire saves your progress.


Controls

Move: A / D or Left / Right arrows

Jump: Space, W or Up arrow

Double jump: press jump again in the air

Attack: J or Left Click

Pause: Esc or P

Mute / unmute: M
