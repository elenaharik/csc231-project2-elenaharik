
                                         The Herbalist's Adventure

                           
Elizabeth Breyer has always been better with herbs than with blades.
Her little shop sells potions, remedies and dried herbs to the whole village, 
and every ingredient comes from the forest nearby.

But lately the forest has changed. Angry mushrooms walk the paths and strange flying creatures guard the trees.
So Elizabeth taught herself to use a sword. Now she ventures into the forest to gather the herbs she needs,
fights off anything that gets in her way, and returns home through the old portal before nightfall.

                                       Controls

Move: A / D or Left / Right arrows
Jump: Space, W or Up arrow
Double jump: press jump again in the air
Attack: J or Left Click
Pause: Esc or P
Mute: M
